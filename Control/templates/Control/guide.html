HR — Employees & Onboarding

Employee directory

List with filters: status, department, role. Click for detail: profile, documents, onboarding checklist, performance history.

Add employee (creates login + onboarding)

  1. Employees → New — fill personal, professional, role, department, manager.
  2. System creates Django user, sends welcome email with temp password.
  3. Onboarding checklist auto-generated from Workflows matching role/department.

Onboarding board

Kanban: Pending → In progress → Submitted → Approved. HR/manager approve steps marked needs approval.

Workflows (templates)

Define checklist templates per role/department. Steps: title, description, assignee (self/HR/manager), approval required, order. Matching priority: role → department → default.

Documents

Upload contracts, ID proofs, offer letters per employee. Categories: contract, id, certificate, other.

TV Displays (Live Leaderboard)

  1. Super Admin → TV displays → New.
  2. Name it (e.g. "Main floor TV"), choose rotation interval (default 10s).
  3. System generates a token URL: /tv//.
  4. Open that URL on the office TV browser (full-screen, kiosk mode).
  5. It cycles through employees showing name, role, photo, score, rank, top KPIs.

Security: Anyone with the link can view — keep it on internal network only.

My onboarding checklist

Open My onboarding and finish each step. Document steps need a file upload.

  • Education certificates are four separate steps: High school, Higher secondary, Graduation and Post-graduation. If one does not apply to you, press Skip.
  • Experience letters are three separate steps: Latest employer, Previous employer I and Previous employer II. Freshers press I'm a fresher - skip (HR may also mark you as a fresher and these are skipped for you).

Growth engine (prospecting & outreach)

The Growth engine finds and warms up prospects before they become CRM leads. Nothing is ever emailed without a person approving it.

Add prospects

Import leads (Excel/CSV) reads an .xlsx or .csv file, recognises common column names (company, contact, email, phone, website, city), shows a preview and skips duplicates of existing prospects and CRM leads. You can also add one prospect by hand from Prospects.

Scan a website for signals

On a prospect (or with Scan selected on the list) the system reads the public homepage and records facts such as "no online booking", "no structured data (schema)", "running Google or Meta ads", "stale blog" and the website platform. Each prospect gets a fit score from 0 to 10 and a plain-English reason. Only signals you have confirmed under Signals & settings are collected and used.

Cohorts and campaigns

The Cohort builder counts prospects live: choose what they must have, what disqualifies them, a minimum score and a city. Press Create campaign to turn the cohort into a campaign with a three-step email sequence (first email, follow-up, last note). The opening line of each email is written from the prospect's strongest signal. Emails stay short and follow the no-pitch-in-the-first-email rule.

Approval desk

Every drafted email waits on the Approval desk. One email fills the pane; use A to approve, R to reject and J/K to move. Approved emails are sent within your daily cap, never to someone who replied or unsubscribed, and always with an unsubscribe link and your company address.

Replies and conversion

When a prospect replies, open the prospect and use Log reply. The system classifies the reply (interested, not interested, unsubscribe, out of office, bounce), stops queued emails and updates the status. For an interested prospect press Convert to CRM lead; the prospect's signals and history move into the lead notes.

FAQ & Troubleshooting

Camera/location permission denied?
Browser settings → Site permissions → Allow camera & location for this origin. Refresh page.
Punch flagged "Outside radius"?
Check office coordinates in Admin → Office locations. Increase radius or correct GPS.
Face recognition fails?
Good lighting, face centred, no mask/glasses. HR can "Reset face" for re-enrolment.
Lead not visible?
Scope is role-based: Executives see own, Team Leads see team, Managers+ see all.
Performance score not updating?
KPIs recalculate on data entry. Force recalc: Admin → BDE → Recalculate.
TV display shows "Unauthorized"?
Token expired or revoked. Generate new token in TV displays.
Forgot password?
Admin can reset in Control Center; or use "Forgot password" on login (if email configured).
